SMS Opt-In Policy & Terms

Luxtravco offers transactional text-message updates to keep customers informed about booking inquiries, reservation status, payment requests, receipts, chauffeur assignments, and account security.

1. Consent and Opt-In

By providing your phone number and checking the SMS agreement box, you expressly consent to receive automated text messages from Luxtravco about your bookings and account. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

2. Message Types and Frequency

Messages may include request-received updates, payment links, payment confirmations, ride reminders, chauffeur updates, and verification codes. Message frequency varies based on your booking activity.

3. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

4. Opting Out and Help

You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or CANCEL. Reply HELP for assistance. To opt in again, reply START, YES, RESUME, or SUBSCRIBE.

5. Privacy

Luxtravco does not share, sell, rent, or lease customer phone numbers or SMS consent details to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

For assistance, contact info@luxtravco.com.
