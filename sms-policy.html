SMS Opt-In Policy & Terms

Luxtravco ("we," "us," or "our") offers transactional SMS message updates to keep our customers informed about their booking inquiries, confirmation details, reservation status, receipts, and professional chauffeur assignments. Please review the terms below to understand how our SMS program operates.

1. Consent and Opt-In

By providing your phone number on our website's reservation request form, via our mobile application, or by initiating a message to our number, you explicitly consent to receive automated text messages from Luxtravco regarding your bookings.

Consent is collected when you enter your contact number on the reservation request form and click "Submit Request." Opt-in consent is unique to you and is not shared with or transferred to any third party.

2. Program Description

Luxtravco SMS notifications include:

  • Booking inquiry updates and confirmations.
  • Payment links and receipts.
  • Trip status updates (e.g., driver on the way, arrival notifications).
  • Customer support communication related directly to your requested rides.

3. Message Frequency and Pricing

Message frequency varies based on your active bookings and interaction levels with our team. Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

4. Opt-Out (Unsubscribe)

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Simply text STOP, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or CANCEL to our number.

After you send the message STOP, we will update your customer profile immediately to opt you out. We will send you one reply message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed, and after that, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us unless you opt back in.

5. Resubscribe (Opt-In again)

If you want to start receiving messages again, reply START, YES, RESUME, or SUBSCRIBE to our number, and we will reactivate SMS messaging for your profile.

6. Help and Customer Support

If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program or need assistance, reply HELP to our number, or contact our support team directly at info@luxtravco.com or call (909) 235‑0670.

7. Privacy Policy & Data Security

We value your privacy. Consumer phone numbers and consent details collected for the purpose of SMS messaging will never be shared, sold, rented, or leased to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is kept strictly confidential and used solely to fulfill your transportation requests.

8. Carrier Disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
